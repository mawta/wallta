Automatic wallpaper rotation

Use your own photos as wallpapers on Windows and Android, or as a screensaver on Android TV. Choose photo sources and set when your pictures change.

Download Wallta Setup guide

Photo sources

Use local folders, image links or connected cloud sources. Wallta can preview photos, rotate them on a schedule and save upscaled copies on your device.

  • Google Drive. Connect an account to browse folders. Wallta reads images from the folders you select for preview and wallpaper rotation.
  • Google Photos. Use Google Photos Picker to choose pictures to import. Wallta stores copies of your selected photos on your device. Android phones use Google's account chooser; TV sign-in uses a QR code on your phone.

Google sign-in is required only when using a Google integration that requests access to your account. See what Wallta accesses, where it is stored and how to remove it in the privacy policy.
