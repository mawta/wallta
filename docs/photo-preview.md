# Photo preview and comparison

Click a photo in Sources, or the expand button on the current photo, to open a large preview. Use the previous/next controls to browse. **100%** displays the served image at its actual pixel size; **Fit to window** returns to the fitted view. Both comparison layers share the same zoom and scroll position.

Newly upscaled photos remember their original source and photo identity. Drag the divider horizontally to reveal **Before** on the left and **After** on the right. Touch is supported. With a keyboard or TV remote, move focus into the comparison control, then use Left/Right to adjust the divider; Up/Down moves to other controls. In the zoomed viewing area, arrows pan the photo. Escape/Back closes the preview.

The large preview reads the actual saved 4K output instead of enlarging the compact job thumbnail. Very large originals are bounded for safe display on the device. The preview reports the dimensions of the image it serves.

Original photos are fetched only when opening a comparison; Wallta does not duplicate the whole source library. A disabled source can still supply the original for this explicit action without becoming a wallpaper source. Removed photos, disconnected accounts or network restrictions may prevent comparison; the saved result stays viewable. Existing collections without original associations offer result preview only. Wallta does not guess an original from an output filename.

Clearing cache keeps saved results and their original associations. Removing an upscaled collection removes its saved results and associated metadata, while leaving source originals untouched.
