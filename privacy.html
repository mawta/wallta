Privacy policy

This policy explains how Wallta uses Google data when you choose to connect a Google source. Wallta is a wallpaper and screensaver app for Windows, Android and Android TV.

Data Wallta accesses

  • Google Drive. The read-only OAuth permission can access Drive files. Wallta uses it to browse folders, list image names and metadata in the folders you select, and read image contents for wallpaper rotation and preview.
  • Google Photos Picker. Wallta uses the Picker to access the image files and metadata you explicitly select on Windows, Android and Android TV. It uses basic Google account identity, including email when provided, to identify the connection in the app. Imported images are saved as local copies; existing albums are not continuously synchronized.
  • Android system photo picker. Wallta receives only the images you choose in the system picker. Available photo providers depend on your device.
  • Google Photos Ambient on supported TVs. This optional integration requires an approved Google Photos partner configuration. It accesses media made available for the connected device to display as a slideshow.

Use, storage and sharing

Wallta uses Google data for the photo features you choose. Settings, source details, image caches, imports and saved upscaled images are stored on your device. Windows stores saved OAuth credentials in Windows Credential Manager. Android uses protected app storage for saved connection credentials.

Windows connects to Google directly and stores saved OAuth credentials in Windows Credential Manager. On Android phones with Google Play services, the system account chooser and Google authorization service grant access to the selected account. Wallta requests renewed access from that service when needed and protects saved connection details with Android Keystore.

On Android TV, or when using QR sign-in, Wallta's Google Apps Script authorization service handles the browser consent and stores the connected account's email, access token and refresh token in private Script Properties. The service operator and trusted script editors can access this storage. The device stores an encrypted connection capability; it can request short-lived Google access tokens, but receives neither the refresh token nor the Web OAuth client secret. Pictures and folder listings travel directly between Google and your device; this authorization service does not proxy or store your pictures. Connections expire after thirty days without successful use. Pending sign-in codes expire after ten minutes.

Older versions and custom builds may use an optional self-hosted Drive relay. That relay handles authorization, stores encrypted tokens and passes requested images from the folder confirmed during pairing. Its operator controls its hosting and operational policies.

Upscaling processes pictures on your device. Wallta uses Google data to provide the requested photo features and does not sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. Wallta’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the Google Photos API User Data and Developer Policy where applicable.

Disconnect and delete data

Remove a connected source in Wallta to stop using it. For QR connections, Wallta also requests deletion of that device's authorization record from the Apps Script service. If the device is offline, reconnect and remove the source before uninstalling, or contact support to request deletion; unused service records expire after thirty days. Cancelling an unsaved QR connection requests deletion of its pending or newly issued grant. In Settings, clear the image cache or remove saved upscaled collections. Deleting a managed imported source removes its local imported copies. Files saved or exported separately can be deleted separately. These actions leave the originals in your Google account.

You can revoke Wallta’s access in Google Account permissions. Revoking Google access stops future authorized requests. To remove copies already saved to your device, use the local controls described above.

Contact

For help or questions about your data, use the support email shown in the Google consent screen or Wallta support on GitHub. Avoid including account credentials or private images in a public issue.
