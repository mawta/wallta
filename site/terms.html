Using Wallta

Terms of use

These terms describe the Wallta wallpaper and screensaver app and the optional services it connects to.

Effective November 27, 2026 · Wallta by mawta

What Wallta does

Wallta displays photos you choose as wallpapers on supported Windows and Android devices, and as a screensaver on supported Android TV devices. You can use local sources without connecting Google. Google Drive and Google Photos features require your separate permission and a working connection. Device firmware, available codecs, permissions and third-party services affect which features work.

Your photos and accounts

You keep the rights to your photos and are responsible for choosing images you are allowed to use. Wallta does not claim ownership of your originals. You control which local or Google sources to connect, can remove those connections, and can revoke Google access in your account settings. See the privacy policy for access, storage and deletion details.

Availability and changes

Wallta depends on operating-system capabilities and, for optional connected features, Google or other services. Features may be delayed or unavailable when those services or your device do not support them. Updates may change features or these terms; the current version will remain available on this site with its effective date.

Third-party services

Google Drive, Google Photos, GitHub downloads and any optional WebDAV or self-hosted relay are separate services with their own terms. Wallta is an independent app and is not endorsed by Google.

Support

For help, use Wallta support on GitHub. For a private data request, use the developer support email shown on Wallta's Google consent screen; do not publish personal information in a GitHub issue.
