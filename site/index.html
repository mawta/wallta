Wallpapers & screensavers

Your photos, on your screens.

Wallta rotates photos from folders you choose on Windows and Android, and plays them as a screensaver on Android TV. Set a schedule, preview your collection and keep the images that matter close.

Choose your sources

Start with your own collection

Local folders and image links work without a Google account. Connect Google only if you want to use images from your Google services.

Local photos

Use folders, supported USB storage, image links or WebDAV/NAS sources. Wallta previews and rotates images on your device.

Google Drive

Grant read-only access, then choose the folders Wallta should browse for wallpaper images. Wallta does not edit or delete your Drive originals.

Google Photos

Use Google Photos Picker to select specific photos. Wallta imports local copies of those selections; it does not continuously sync an album.

Google data & privacy

Clear choices for connected accounts

Wallta requests Google permissions only when you choose a Google feature. Drive access lets the app read files and folder details for the folders you select. Photos Picker grants access to the photos you pick. Some devices use a QR sign-in service that stores authorization tokens to keep the connection working; images travel directly from Google to your device.

Read the full details before connecting.

Our privacy policy explains the exact Google data used, where tokens and imported images are stored, how long QR connections remain, and how to disconnect or delete data. You can also read the terms of use.

The developer also uses the same OAuth project's Desktop client for a separate, personal encrypted backup workflow in the developer's own Drive account. This is not a feature of Wallta for other users; the privacy policy discloses that use.
